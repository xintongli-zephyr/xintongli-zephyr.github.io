    Xintong Li

    I transferred to SCI-Arc from the School of Architecture at Southeast University, and 2B is my first semester here. I came from an education built on classical, traditional ways of making space, and expected SCI-Arc to pull entirely the other way. Instead it has asked me to understand architecture itself: construction, structure, plans and the history of modern thinking, while tools like Grasshopper let environmental forces become part of a building's surface and structure.

    I hope to become a designer who looks back carefully at architectural traditions while staying open to the future.
